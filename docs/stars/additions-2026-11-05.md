## Boundary decisions

- Domain-specific skills follow their domain: Unreal → Games & 3D; video skills → Image, Video & Presentations; design systems → UI & Product Design.
- HarnessRouter and Mindwire adapt existing harnesses for products → Agent Tools & Environments, rather than model gateways or new agent loops.
- Nimbus authors developer documentation; djs is a language engine → Developer Tools & Languages.
- ai-design-check, Desloppify, AnnotateKit and Counterpoint inspect or improve software quality → Testing, Debugging & Code Quality.
- FlashProve verifies model identity cryptographically; OSS-Fuzz explicitly targets security vulnerabilities and stability → Security, Privacy & Identity.
- Jitsu and InsightFlare are general data pipelines/analytics → Data, Databases & GIS. icloud-s3 is an experimental object store explicitly described as satire, not a production storage recommendation.
- Rerun and Mercury are general assistants/orchestration platforms. Invoice retrieval, signatures and cold email follow their business domains.
- ai-cli is a general SDK-backed multimodal generation/typed-decision interface → AI Engineering & Learning, without a dominant audio or video specialization.
